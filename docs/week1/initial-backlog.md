# Initial Backlog Ideas

The initial product direction was a private reading tracker that could grow into a community discovery service. The rough ideas were refined into nine formal Stories after interviews.

1. Secure reader accounts and private data.
2. Search an external book catalogue instead of retyping metadata.
3. Maintain a personal shelf and simple reading statuses.
4. Discover books by genre, subject or reader-created traits.
5. Publish ratings and reviews.
6. Curate public or private book lists and profiles.
7. Discuss a book through posts and threaded replies.
8. Receive recommendations and dismiss unwanted suggestions.
9. Moderate shared content and deploy a dependable live service.

The formal priorities, estimates, acceptance criteria and iteration allocation are in [`../week2/requirements-backlog.md`](../week2/requirements-backlog.md).
